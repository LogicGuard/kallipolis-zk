# Kallipolis ZK: The Decentralized Mempool Perimeter

## Our Philosophy
We believe that the mempool is the most underserved layer in the blockchain stack. Kallipolis is not just a tool; it is a fundamental shift in how we approach transaction integrity.

## Our Core Beliefs
1. **Security by Default**: Security is not an add-on; it is the substrate.
2. **Polyglot Modularity**: The best tool for the job. We use Rust for speed, Zig for memory safety, and OCaml for formal correctness.
3. **ZK-First**: Trust is verified, never assumed.
4. **Open Governance**: The future of AggLayer security belongs to the community.

## The Mission
To build the most resilient, high-throughput, and formally-verified mempool firewall, ensuring that the Polygon ecosystem remains impenetrable to front-running, sandwich attacks, and malicious transaction flooding.
